What this is

Ginger Snaps Dashboard is an internal reporting tool operated by Ginger Snaps Media. It is used only for accounts that Ginger Snaps Media owns or manages, with the account owner's permission.

Connecting an account

By connecting your Instagram or TikTok account, you allow Ginger Snaps Media to read your account's profile details and performance statistics in order to prepare reports for you. The tool has read-only access and never posts or changes anything on your account.

Disconnecting

You can remove access at any time, from your Instagram or TikTok app settings or by asking us. See our Privacy Policy for how data is stored and deleted.

No warranty

Reports are based on the statistics Instagram and TikTok provide, which can be delayed or revised by those platforms. They are provided as-is, for information only.
