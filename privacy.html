Privacy Policy

Ginger Snaps Dashboard · Last updated October 16, 2026

Ginger Snaps Dashboard is a private reporting tool used by Ginger Snaps Media, a social media management business in Charlottesville, Virginia, to measure the performance of social media accounts it manages. It is not a public app, and it isn't offered to anyone else.

What we access

When an account owner chooses to connect their Instagram or TikTok account, we receive read-only access to that account's public profile details (name, username, profile picture), follower, following, like and video counts, and statistics for the account's own posts and videos, such as views, likes, comments, shares and saves. We do not access private messages, and we do not post, comment or make any changes on the connected account.

How we use it

This information is used only to show performance trends to Ginger Snaps Media and to prepare monthly performance reports for the owner of the connected account. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Statistics are stored only on a private computer used by Ginger Snaps Media. Login credentials issued by Instagram or TikTok are kept in that computer's encrypted system keychain. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep statistics for as long as we manage the account, so that growth can be compared over time. You can disconnect at any time, from your Instagram settings (Apps and websites) or TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), or by asking us. When you ask, we delete the stored statistics and credentials for your account.
